🌱 School Projects - Pfumvudza Learning by Doing

1. Poultry (Huku): 100 broilers + 50 layers. Feeding, vaccination, records. Maths: cost vs sales. Profit for books. Status: Term1 2027
2. Fisheries (Hove): 2 Tilapia ponds, borehole water. Fish farming business. Agriculture + Science. Site near Bonda dam.
3. Apiculture - Beekeeping (Nyuchi): 10 hives, honey, pollination, environment. Heritage respect. Agritex training requested.
4. Gardening (Muriwo): 30x20m fenced - Rape, Covo, Tomato, Onion. Feeds ECD learners. Science practical.
5. Field Crops (Minda): 1ha Maize, 0.5ha Groundnuts, Sorghum. Pfumvudza heritage grains. Land allocated by Chief Bonda.
